# Contributing to Breadslicer

Breadslicer is in early development and the repository is private for now.

- **Tasks** live in the [issues](https://github.com/breadslicer-app/breadslicer/issues) as
  `BS-<number>: title`, labelled by area and state.
- **Ideas** are welcome as issues labelled `idea`; they get sorted, split and linked before work starts.
- **Changes** go in micro-PRs: one issue (or one small part of it) per PR, on a branch named
  `issue/<number>-<slug>`, saying what changed, why, how it was tested, and `Closes #<number>`.

The full workflow is in [`docs/WORKFLOW.md`](docs/WORKFLOW.md).

By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed under the
[AGPL-3.0](LICENSE.md), like the rest of the project.
