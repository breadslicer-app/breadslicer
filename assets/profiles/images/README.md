# Printer product images

Product photos for the bundled printer profiles, vendored unmodified
from the printer plugin repositories of the DragonFruit slicer,
published by the Open Resin Alliance:

- <https://github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance/df-plugin-anycubic>
  (`printers/assets/`): the Anycubic printers.
- <https://github.com/Open-Resin-Alliance/df-plugin-elegoo>
  (`printers/assets/`): the Elegoo printers.

Both repositories are published under the MIT license with identical
license texts, reproduced verbatim in `LICENSE-MIT.txt`. Every image
in this directory was verified byte-identical to its upstream
counterpart on 2026-08-02, and `elegoo-mars-2-pro.png` on 2026-09-10;
only the file names differ, renamed to match this repository's printer
profile ids.

Upstream has no dedicated photo for 3 of the bundled printer
variants; their files are copies of a sibling model's image:

- `elegoo-mars-3-pro.png` reuses the Mars 3 image.
- `elegoo-saturn-2-14k.png` reuses the Saturn 2 image.
- `elegoo-mars-4-dlp.png` reuses the Mars 4 Max image.

The photos depict hardware by Anycubic and Elegoo; product names are
trademarks of their respective owners.
