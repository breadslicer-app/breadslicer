Breadslicer {version} for Linux (x86_64)
========================================

Run it from this folder, nothing to install:

    ./bin/breadslicer

To add it to your desktop's menu, with its icon and its file types (.bread,
.stl, .obj, .3mf), for your account only:

    ./install-desktop-entry.sh

(`./install-desktop-entry.sh --remove` takes it out again.)

bin/sc-slicer-cli is the command-line slicer.

Needs a graphics driver with Vulkan (Mesa on AMD and Intel, the NVIDIA
driver on NVIDIA) and glibc 2.35 or newer.
